Common questions

Who can use Lendr?

Students at a supported university. Sign up with your school email and confirm it from your inbox. Your campus is added automatically, and you get a Verified student badge.

I didn't get the confirmation email.

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you typed your school email correctly. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, email us.

How does renting work?

Find an item in Browse, choose your dates and tap Request to borrow. Once the lender approves, you can see each other's pickup contact to arrange a time. The lender takes a photo at pickup and again at return, and both of you can see those photos.

How do deposits and payments work?

Lendr doesn't process payments yet. Prices and deposits are shown on each listing, and you arrange payment directly with the other student. The deposit should be returned when the item comes back in the same condition.

How do I report something or block someone?

Open the listing, Wanted post or rental and tap Report, then choose a reason. To block someone, tap Block user on their listing or Wanted post. You can unblock people in Settings under Blocked users. We review every report within 24 hours.

What isn't allowed?

Illegal, dangerous or stolen items, weapons, drugs or alcohol, scams, harassment, and offensive content. We remove content and accounts that break these rules.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, tap the settings gear, and tap Delete account. If you have an item that's currently picked up, return it first. Deleting your account removes your profile, listings and rental history, and can't be undone.

Safety tips

  • Meet in busy, public spots on campus, like a residence hall lobby or the library.
  • Check the item together before handing it over, and let the lender take the pickup photo.
  • Keep conversations respectful, and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
