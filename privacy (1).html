Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Lendr is a campus app where university students lend and borrow everyday items from each other. It is built and run by Debra Anumnu, a student developer in Grand Rapids, Michigan. This policy explains what information Lendr collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect what the app needs to work, like your name, school email, listings and rentals. We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, school email address and password. Your password is stored securely by our login provider and we can't see it.
  • Profile photo, if you add one.
  • Listings: item names, descriptions, categories, condition, prices, deposits, pickup spots and photos.
  • Rentals: the items you request or lend, dates, amounts, and the status of each rental.
  • Handoff photos: photos a lender takes when an item is picked up and returned, and any photos of damage a lender adds when reporting a problem after a return.
  • Deposit and damage records: whether a renter says they got their deposit back, and any damage claim, including the reason, the amount kept, the description, and the renter's response.
  • Pickup contact: a phone number, social media handle or email you choose to add for arranging pickups.
  • Wanted posts and star ratings you leave after a rental.
  • Reports and blocks: content or people you report, the reason you give, and people you block.
  • Messages you send us when you contact support.

Information created automatically

  • Campus and verification: your campus is filled in from your school email's domain, and your account is marked as a verified student once you confirm that email.
  • Activity totals shown on your profile, such as your rating, Lendr points and earnings, which are calculated from your rentals.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, device or browser type, and the time of requests, to keep the service running and secure.

Lendr does not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment card details, and does not use advertising or third-party tracking tools.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, and confirm you are a student at a supported campus.
  • To show listings and Wanted posts, and to manage rental requests, approvals, pickups and returns.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks the rules, and enforcing blocks.
  • To help resolve disagreements between users, for example about the condition of a returned item or a deposit, using handoff and damage photos, damage claims and rental records.
  • To answer your questions and fix problems with the app.

Who can see your information

  • Other Lendr users can see your name, profile photo, campus, verified status, rating, listings and Wanted posts.
  • Your pickup contact is only shown to someone you have an approved or in-progress rental with, and it is hidden again once the item is returned.
  • Handoff photos, damage photos, damage claims and rental details are only visible to the lender and renter of that rental, and to Lendr's moderators when a dispute is reported.
  • Reports are only visible to the person who filed them and to Lendr's moderators. The person you report is not told who reported them.
  • People you block no longer see your listings or Wanted posts, and you no longer see theirs.

We share information with the following service providers only so they can run Lendr for us: Supabase (database, login, file storage and hosting) and Apple (app distribution). We may also share information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or others.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Payments

Lendr does not currently process payments. Prices and deposits are shown in the app, and lenders and renters arrange payment directly with each other. If in-app payments are added later, this policy will be updated first.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. Handoff and damage photos stay attached to the rental as a record, even if a damage claim is withdrawn, so they can be used if a dispute comes up later. When you delete your account, your profile, listings, rentals, ratings, Wanted posts, pickup contact, reports and blocks are deleted, and photos you uploaded are removed within 30 days. Standard server logs are kept for a limited time by our hosting provider.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, photo and pickup contact at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in Settings. This can't be undone.
  • Block anyone, and report content or people that break the rules.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing danumnu02@gmail.com.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and access rules in our database limit each user to the information they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

Age

Lendr is intended for university students. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and let users know in the app.

Contact
